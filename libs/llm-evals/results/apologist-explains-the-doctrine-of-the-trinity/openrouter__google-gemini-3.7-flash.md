>

> ### 2. God is Three in Person
>
> While God is one divine Being, Scripture reveals three distinct Persons who are each fully God:
>
> - **The Father** is God (1 Corinthians 8:6).

> - **The Holy Spirit** is God (Acts 5:3–4).
>

>
> Notice that "name" is singular, affirming one God, yet followed by three distinct Persons.
>

>
> Historically, the Church has guarded this mystery against two main errors:

> - **Tritheism:** The false idea that there are three separate gods. The three Persons share the exact same divine essence, not three separate pieces of divinity.
