> ### 2. Is It Still Happening Today?
>
> Christians hold two primary views regarding whether the gift continues today:
>
> - **Continuationism:** This view holds that all gifts of the Holy Spirit, including tongues, are active today and will continue until Christ returns. Proponents point to Paul’s command, *"do not forbid speaking in tongues"* (1 Corinthians 14:39), and argue that the New Testament does not teach that miraculous gifts would end before the second coming.
> - **Cessationism:** This view holds that miraculous sign gifts, such as tongues and prophetic revelation, were unique to the apostolic era. Their purpose was to authenticate the apostles' message and lay the foundation of the Church while the New Testament canon was being completed (Ephesians 2:20; Hebrews 2:3–4; 1 Corinthians 13:8).
